Research Update on Safer Text-to-Image Generation

December 2, 2025

Our work studies targeted interventions in text embedding spaces to reduce unsafe image generation while preserving useful model behavior.

This research reflects our interest in understanding and steering generative models rather than treating them only as black-box systems.
