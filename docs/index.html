Safe & Applied
Intelligence Lab

SAIL pursues two broad directions in artificial intelligence. We study trustworthy AI to understand, steer, and improve learning systems, including their internal mechanisms and unwanted behaviors. We also develop practical AI for real-world domains, exploring vision, multimodal and generative models, and field-ready solutions under real operating constraints.
